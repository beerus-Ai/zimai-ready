Preparing Zimbabwe's workforce
for the age of AI.

ZimAI Ready is a web platform that tells employees how AI affects their job, teaches them the skills that matter for their profession, and certifies them — while giving employers a clear, data-driven view of how AI-ready their organisation is and where to invest in reskilling.

The problem we solve

Everyone is told to "learn AI", but most professionals and organisations have no way to answer the basic questions:

  • How exposed is my role to AI — and am I prepared?
  • Which AI skills matter in my profession, and what should I learn first?
  • Should I upskill in my role or reskill for a new one?
  • How can I prove I can use AI responsibly at work?
  • For employers: which teams are ready, and where should training money go?
Built for Zimbabwe's key sectors

How it works — five steps, one pathway

01
Assess

A 2-minute, 9-question check-in on role, sector, tasks, AI use and confidence.

02
Discover

Personal AI Readiness score, role AI-exposure, and a prioritised AI Skills Prescription.

03
Learn

Personalised micro-lessons for 11 professional domains, with quick checks and an AI Tutor.

04
Practise

Realistic workplace scenarios scored by AI against a transparent rubric.

05
Certify

Knowledge test + capstone earn AI Aware, Capable or Ready — publicly verifiable, renewed yearly.

For employees

  • Clean dashboard: one next step and four key numbers
  • Career-transition analysis (e.g. clerk → data analyst)
  • AI Tutor that knows the learner's role and progress
  • Shareable skills profile and QR-verifiable certificates

For employers

  • Workforce readiness verdict by department
  • AI exposure vs readiness and skills-gap analysis
  • Organisational AI maturity score and competencies
  • AI Workforce Advisor recommending where to invest

How it's built

  • React 19 + TypeScript + Vite, Tailwind design system
  • Google Gemini for assessment, tutoring and feedback
  • Firebase: Google sign-in and Cloud Firestore with security rules
  • Deployed on Google AI Studio; offline demo mode included
